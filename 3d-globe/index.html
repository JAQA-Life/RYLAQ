JAQA Eye View

A photorealistic 3D globe of live public signals, part of JAQA.

Open the 3D globe

What it shows

  • Photorealistic Earth. Satellite imagery and OpenStreetMap without keys; photorealistic 3D city tiles when the server has a Google Maps key or Cesium ion token.
  • Live contacts. Aircraft, ships and satellites, with click-to-track, trails and a 250 km contacts roster.
  • Hazards and weather. Earthquakes, wildfires, weather radar, wind, clouds, lightning and tropical cyclone tracks.
  • Space. Satellite orbits, pass predictions and a rolling 30 days of launches.
  • Cameras and infrastructure. Public CCTV cameras, mapped ALPR camera locations, datacenters, dams, submarine cables and military installations.
  • Sensor looks. CRT, NVG, FLIR/thermal, Noir and Snow styles, a detection overlay and a tactical HUD.
  • Voice control. Hands-free control through the OpenAI Realtime API when the server has an OpenAI key.

From the workspace

The Situation Room, the Global Monitor and the 3D Globe screen in the workspace open the globe at the place you are looking at. Links carry only coordinates and a style, never your search.

Data and licences

Each map layer’s source is credited under “Data attribution” on the globe. Open-source licences are on the Licences page. Positions, feeds and camera images can be delayed, incomplete or wrong; nothing on the globe is a determination of risk, ownership, intent or exposure.
